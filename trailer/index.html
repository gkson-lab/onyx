Your own AI team, on your own PC.

Onyx Agents runs open-weight language models on your PC through Ollama. A message goes to Onyx Core, which answers it or hands parts of it to specialists (a Researcher, a Coder, a Scheduler, a Messenger and a Memory agent) and then writes one answer. No subscription, no charge per message, and your conversations stay on your machine.

Filmed from the real app, on a 4-core processor with no graphics card. The waits for the model are sped up, and each sped-up part says by how much. The video has no sound.
