Thinks in steps you can follow.

Onyx 3 runs in this page. It plans what you ask, shows its thinking with every reply, writes text and code from a prompt, and has dictionaries for 31 languages besides English. It goes online only when the internet switch says it may.

Internet

Off. Everything stays on this device.

When on, Onyx can ask Wikipedia, Open-Meteo and the Free Dictionary API, and sends only the words to look up.

Thinking

The steps behind each reply appear here.

    Documents

    Give Onyx a text file, or paste a long text into the chat, then ask about it.

    Voice

    31 dictionaries

    Onyx talks in English, Spanish and French, and translates words between all of these. Each dictionary loads the first time it is asked for.

    Free to use

    Onyx 3 is free and open source. No account, no key, no tracking: it runs in this page, on your device.
